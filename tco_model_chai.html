Key Model Uncertainties: The Two Least Confident Variables
1. Effective Battery Lifespan in Field Conditions (Base: 36 Months) The supplier claims a 48 to 60 month lifespan based on 1,200 cycles in controlled conditions. However, high ambient temperatures above 40°C in states like Rajasthan and Gujarat degrade batteries significantly faster than laboratory ratings. Our model applies a conservative one-third haircut down to 36 months, but full real-world lifespan data is not yet proven.
2. Number of Batteries per Tower (Base: 6 Units) Tower configurations vary widely across networks: 2G/3G sites typically use 4 battery units, while heavier 4G sites use 6 to 8 units. The exact string size across the operator's fleet is unconfirmed. While this variable scales the total rupee savings, our sensitivity analysis proves it does not alter the break-even lifespan threshold.

Operational Assumptions

Structural Modeling Rules

# Operational Assumption
1 All battery units at a tower site are replaced together in a single technician visit at the end of each operating cycle.
2 Batteries are replaced when usable capacity drops below the operator's working threshold, which is identical for both options.
3 Financial figures are evaluated on a nominal annual basis without general price inflation or discounting.
4 Scrap income is earned for every replaced battery unit and is identical per unit for both battery options.
5 Technician labor, transport, and equipment costs per replacement visit are identical for installing both types of batteries.
6 Batteries are assumed to achieve their stated operating life on average without premature random cell failures.
7 Both battery types have a 150Ah capacity rating and deliver equivalent usable power backup to the telecom tower.
8 Environmental and health impacts from battery recycling are excluded to keep the financial evaluation strictly focused on procurement costs.
9 Taxes, freight, and standard installation are excluded from the model and assumed equal across both battery options.
10 The standard battery lifespan of 20 months in high-temperature regions is applied across the evaluation fleet.
11 The bulk purchase price of ₹17,200 applies to the full 10,000-tower deployment because total order volume exceeds 5,000 units.
12 Performance data from ongoing field deployments is not used to adjust the baseline lifespan assumption until complete failure data is available.
